The October 5 revision keeps the coin stacks and ascending budget bars, with natural champagne gold coins, sage bars and a forest-green tile based on the app colour `#285a44`. It is not cropped from the reference sheet.

All 14 app icons, logos, favicons and social exports are listed in `exports.json`. Existing public filenames are retained for compatibility and their old artwork is replaced. SVG files embed the new raster illustration, rather than editable vector shapes.

Rebuild with `python scripts/build_brand.py` (Pillow and Playwright Chromium required). The exporter preserves the complete illustration and transparency, resizes icons, and lays out the app name for wordmarks. Social backgrounds use `#285a44`. Application styling is unchanged.
